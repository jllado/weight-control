# Food nutrients

Foods, recipe ingredients and logged food snapshots carry vitamin D (µg), legacy total omega-3 (mg), magnesium (mg), EPA+DHA (mg), ALA (mg) and fiber (g). EPA+DHA, ALA and fiber each have their own nullable value, reference, source note and estimate flag; a known zero needs explicit evidence, while a missing value remains unknown. Manual and Coach food writes still require vitamin D, magnesium and their source. Legacy total omega-3 stays independent and must never stand in for EPA+DHA or ALA.

Portion references preserve each value independently. Quantity changes scale with half-up two-decimal rounding; disabling scaling or correcting units resets the reference without implicit conversion. Copies and recipe expansion remain independent snapshots. Meal and day totals sum known values per nutrient and expose independent food and estimate coverage; legacy aggregate counts remain available to old readers. Coverage concerns logged foods only, not all actual consumption. Foodless meals and unknown nutrient fields remain unknown.

Personal settings store a protein minimum, fat percentage, and optional vitamin D, magnesium, EPA+DHA, ALA and fiber targets. Each eligible recorded date uses its effective saved calorie plan or weekday budget to derive fat and carbohydrate ceilings; carbohydrate allocation is the remaining energy after protein and fat. Thirty-day macro rows use completed days with complete recorded macros; micronutrient rows use completed days with full per-field food coverage. Fat and carbohydrate statuses average dated ceilings over exactly their eligible days; a missing or incompatible target stays neutral. Preserve full calculation precision until display rounding. Historical total omega-3 is read-only in the new settings view.

It locks matched rows, validates owner/name/portion/calorie fields and empty destination fields, then fills reference and scaled values transactionally. Failure must disconnect and roll back; never ignore SQL errors. Repeated application rejects already populated values.

Before execution, compare mapping counts with the fresh inventory and record unresolved rows. No live external API is called during migration or food saves.

## Fiber recording and partial totals

A known consumed fiber value requires a known fiber value for its authoritative reference portion; inconsistent writes are rejected before a replacement can discard the value. A label with 8g/100g therefore remains 4g at 50g and 6g at 75g across saves and reloads. Unknown fiber stays null; zero requires source evidence.

Meal and day nutrient values show `≥` when some logged foods lack that nutrient or a meal has no food breakdown. Select a value for its coverage, estimate status and foodless-meal details; an entirely unknown value stays `—`. These are recorded subtotals, not adequacy judgments. Existing trend eligibility and targets are unchanged.

Coach requests FOODS with `foodQuery` for each relevant food; matching is case-insensitive, trimmed, and limited to 20 results with `matchedFoods` indicating whether refinement is needed. Calls without a query retain the full catalog contract. Coach reuses known saved fiber but researches missing fiber from a matching label/composition source, including for existing foods. Any estimate, portion and source is included in the exact meal confirmation. Catalog/recipe templates and historical meals are not silently enriched.

## Separate EPA+DHA historical enrichment

The later additive migrations leave legacy nutrient values and timestamps untouched and create nullable EPA+DHA, ALA and fiber columns. Updated meal, catalog and recipe replacement clients send `nutrientSchemaVersion: 2` with explicit nullable values; an unaware replacement of a snapshot containing any of these nutrients is rejected before mutation, including an empty-list replacement or deleted same-name catalog revival. Old creates and replacements of entirely unknown snapshots remain compatible.

Only the owner's meal-dish snapshots in the 60 Madrid calendar days ending on the execution date are candidates for EPA+DHA enrichment. Keep the full frozen inventory, source evidence, selected manifest and guarded SQL in ignored `tmp/nutrients/`. Use a measured edible mass for nonzero values, except an explicit source-backed zero that is independent of serving mass. Label product or preparation substitutions as estimates; unknown foods stay null. Before applying, refresh the inclusive window if Madrid's date changed, independently reread and byte-compare every frozen old row, then use one transaction that locks owner meals and all in-window dishes, asserts all old states and destination states, updates only selected EPA fields, accepts an exact replay as a no-op, and rejects mixed or stale state. Read back the populated set and compare every unrelated old field after commit. This enrichment does not alter catalog or recipe snapshots and does not backfill ALA or fiber.
