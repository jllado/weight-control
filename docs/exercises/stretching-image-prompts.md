Single adult man in teal short-sleeve shirt, navy shorts, gray trainers, white background, subtle floor shadow. Clear anatomically accurate joints and hands; all limbs visible within frame, no labels, no text, no arrows, no logos. Show one comfortable held position, not extreme range.

## Exercise prompts

The final figure-four, wrist flexor, and bent-knee calf pictures were corrected after visual review; exact edit prompts follow the exercise list.

- **Seated butterfly stretch** (`seated-butterfly-stretch.jpg`): Sit tall with the soles of your feet together and let your knees relax outward; hold your ankles and lean slightly forward from your hips without pressing your knees down.
- **Lying figure-four stretch** (`lying-figure-four-stretch.jpg`): Lie on your back with knees bent; cross one ankle over the opposite thigh and hold behind the supporting thigh to gently draw it toward your chest.
- **Knee-to-chest stretch** (`knee-to-chest-stretch.jpg`): Lie on your back with knees bent and feet flat; hold behind one thigh and gently draw that knee toward your chest while keeping your head relaxed on the floor.
- **Child's pose stretch** (`childs-pose-stretch.jpg`): Kneel on a mat, sit your hips toward your heels, and reach your arms forward as you lower your chest comfortably toward the floor.
- **Doorway chest stretch** (`doorway-chest-stretch.jpg`): Stand beside a doorway with one forearm on the frame and your elbow just below shoulder height; take a small step forward until you feel a gentle stretch across your chest.
- **Overhead triceps stretch** (`overhead-triceps-stretch.jpg`): Raise one arm overhead and bend the elbow so your hand reaches behind your neck; use your other hand to gently guide the raised elbow while keeping your ribs relaxed.
- **Seated hamstring stretch** (`seated-hamstring-stretch.jpg`): Sit near the front of a stable chair with one leg extended and its heel on the floor; keep your back long and lean forward from your hips with your hands resting on the bent-leg thigh.
- **Seated side stretch** (`seated-side-stretch.jpg`): Sit upright on a stable chair with feet flat; raise one arm overhead and lean gently to the opposite side while keeping both hips on the seat.
- **Wrist flexor stretch** (`wrist-flexor-stretch.jpg`): Extend one arm forward with your palm up; use the other hand to gently draw the fingers down and back while keeping the elbow comfortably straight.
- **Wrist extensor stretch** (`wrist-extensor-stretch.jpg`): Extend one arm forward with your palm down; bend the wrist so the fingers point toward the floor and gently guide the back of that hand toward you with your other hand.
- **Bent-knee calf stretch** (`bent-knee-calf-stretch.jpg`): Face a wall with hands supported and one foot a short step behind you; bend both knees gently while keeping the back heel on the floor.
- **Standing inner-thigh stretch** (`standing-inner-thigh-stretch.jpg`): Stand with feet wide and toes forward; bend one knee and shift your hips toward that side while keeping the opposite leg straight and both feet flat.

## Correction prompts

### lying-figure-four-stretch

Correct the pose to a supine figure-four stretch: right ankle rests across LEFT THIGH just above left knee, right knee opens outward. BOTH FEET OFF FLOOR. Both hands clasp BEHIND LEFT THIGH pulling left thigh gently toward chest. Clearly show crossed ankle and outward knee, choose elevated three-quarter camera. Head rests on mat. Preserve style.

### wrist-flexor-stretch

Correct to WRIST EXTENSION, a wrist FLEXOR stretch. The extended forearm must be SUPINATED (palm facing UP before bending wrist). Fingers bend DOWN toward floor, so palm now faces FORWARD away from man, NOT toward his torso. Other hand gently pulls fingers backward toward torso. Show visible palm creases facing viewer, straight elbow. Preserve style.

### bent-knee-calf-stretch

Correct pose: shorten stance, bend BOTH knees visibly about 30 degrees, lowering hips slightly. REAR knee must be clearly BENT toward wall, with rear heel flat on floor. This is bent-knee soleus stretch, not straight-leg calf stretch. Side view showing both knee bends. Preserve style.
