# Yoga and mobility illustrations

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool. Final JPEGs live in `backend/src/main/resources/exercise-images/`; originals remain in the generator output directory.

## Shared prompt

Create a square instructional fitness illustration. Realistic hand-painted style matching a fitness app: adult man in teal short sleeve shirt, navy shorts, gray trainers on a gray yoga mat, pure white background. Full body clearly visible, anatomically correct two arms and two legs, all joints readable, no text or arrows or labels or logos. Show ONE held pose with comfortable moderate range, not extreme flexibility.

Each prompt adds the exercise name and position below, then: Choose a three-quarter or side camera angle that clearly shows the defining joint positions.

- **Lying straight-leg hold** (`lying-straight-leg-hold.jpg`): Lie on your back with one leg resting straight on the mat; raise the other leg with its knee straight and hold without using your hands or a strap. Keep your head relaxed and use a comfortable angle; reaching beyond 90 degrees is not required.
- **All-fours rotation** (`all-fours-rotation.jpg`): Start on hands and knees; keep both knees and one hand grounded, rotate your chest, and reach the other arm toward the ceiling. Keep your hips above your knees and use a comfortable range.
- **Lying spinal twist** (`lying-spinal-twist.jpg`): Lie on your back with arms spread on the floor; bend one knee and let it cross toward the opposite side while the other leg stays extended. Keep your shoulders relaxed; the knee does not need to reach the floor.
- **Seated 90/90 hip stretch** (`seated-90-90-hip-stretch.jpg`): Sit with one leg bent in front and the other bent to the side and behind, with both knees roughly at right angles. Keep your torso long and lean gently over the front leg; use your hands for balance.
- **Downward-facing dog** (`downward-facing-dog.jpg`): From hands and knees, tuck your toes and lift your hips up and back with your hands grounded. Keep your knees softly bent as needed and lengthen your back; your heels do not need to touch the floor.
- **Sphinx pose** (`sphinx-pose.jpg`): Lie face down with your legs extended; place your elbows under your shoulders and rest your forearms on the mat. Gently lift your chest while keeping your pelvis grounded and shoulders relaxed.
- **Extended puppy pose** (`extended-puppy-pose.jpg`): Start on hands and knees; keep your hips above your knees, walk your hands forward, and gently lower your chest. Let your head relax without forcing your chest or forehead to the floor.
- **Happy baby pose** (`happy-baby-pose.jpg`): Lie on your back, bend your knees toward your torso, and hold the outer edges of your feet. Let your knees open beside your torso with soles facing upward; keep your head and shoulders resting on the mat.
- **Seated forward fold** (`seated-forward-fold.jpg`): Sit with your legs together in front of you and knees softly bent as needed. Lengthen your back and lean forward from your hips with your hands resting comfortably on your legs; reaching your feet is not required.
- **Seated wide-leg forward fold** (`seated-wide-leg-forward-fold.jpg`): Sit with your legs comfortably apart and knees softly bent as needed. Lengthen your back and lean forward from your hips with your hands on the floor in front; your chest does not need to reach the floor.

## Review

Check all limbs, joint positions, moderate range, and agreement with the description. The unsupported straight-leg hold uses no hands or strap; the rotation keeps both knees grounded; the twist crosses one bent knee; the 90/90 pose bends both knees.

Reference material: [Yoga Journal pose library](https://www.yogajournal.com/poses/) and [Cleveland Clinic 90/90 stretch](https://health.clevelandclinic.org/90-90-stretch). Reference pictures are not copied into the app.
