# Harmonovo Naming Brief and Screening Note

## Selected identity

- Public name: Harmonovo
- Root domain: harmonovo.com
- Public contact email: hello@harmonovo.com

## Naming brief

Harmonovo is a language-neutral name for a personal health and fitness tracking tool that helps people notice patterns and progress across routines, activity, nutrition, sleep, weight, and habits. The public positioning is "Harmonovo — Health & Fitness"; it describes personal tracking and improvement without making diagnostic, treatment, or medical-outcome claims.

## Initial screening

The candidate review excluded `FitControl` and `HealthControl` because of existing competing uses. An initial exact-match screening of Harmonovo covered general web search, Apple and Google app stores, social networks, GitHub, EUIPO, and the Spanish Patent and Trademark Office. It found no relevant exact product or trademark conflict at the time of review, and `harmonovo.com` was available and registered. This is a practical preliminary screen, not formal trademark clearance; obtain professional advice before a public launch or trademark filing.
