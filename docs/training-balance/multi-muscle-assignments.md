# Multi-muscle assignment defaults

The migration adds role assignments to the 29 reviewed strength exercises in the retained [2026-10-04 catalog snapshot](catalog-2026-10-04.json). A read-only check of the authenticated production catalog on 2026-10-10 confirmed those same 29 strength exercises and classifications. It copies every existing primary classification unchanged, including Weighted dip's later Triceps correction. Catalog rows added later keep the primary classification already required by V98 and receive no guessed secondary assignments. Excluded activities receive no assignments.

Roles and credit are app accounting conventions: each primary assignment contributes 1 equivalent set and each secondary assignment contributes 0.5. They are not measurements of physiological effort, muscle activation, or a prescription. Multiple primaries are allowed and each receives full credit.

All other reviewed strength exercises retain their existing primary assignment without a secondary assignment: Band lateral raise, Banded clamshell, Banded hip abduction, Dead bug, Abdominal crunch, Dumbbell Side Bend, Wall sit, Jefferson curl, Lateral shoulder raise. This avoids implying a secondary muscle when the reviewed movement description or available source did not support a useful assignment for this accounting view. All eleven groups remain visible in the chart, including groups with zero credited sets in a given week.

Sources support target muscles or related movement patterns; they do not prescribe these role labels or the 1.0/0.5 accounting weights. Where the exact catalog variation is not covered, the table marks the role as a movement-pattern extrapolation. These are reviewed categorical defaults for the app's display, not claims about measured activation or equal physiological contribution.
